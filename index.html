Philips Norelco Multigroom 5000

Face, Head and Body — MG5750/49

The Philips Norelco Multigroom 5000 MG5750/49 is an all-in-one grooming system designed for trimming and styling the face, head and body.

The system includes 18 grooming pieces and uses Philips DualCut blade technology. Its interchangeable tools and guards allow one rechargeable trimmer to handle several different grooming jobs.

  • 18-piece all-in-one grooming system
  • DualCut blade technology
  • Face, head and body grooming
  • Beard and stubble trimming guards
  • Hair trimming guards
  • Body trimming guards
  • Nose and ear trimmer attachment
  • Detail metal trimmer
  • Extra-wide hair trimmer
  • Up to 3 hours of cordless runtime
  • No-slip rubber grip
